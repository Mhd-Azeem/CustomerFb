Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-11-27

Information you share

This website has no application form or account. If you send a WhatsApp message, we receive the phone number or profile details WhatsApp shows us, your message and any information or attachments you choose to send. Keep your first message general and leave out passport numbers, identity documents, medical information and bank details.

How we use and share information

We use enquiry details to reply, explain next steps and, if you want to proceed, refer your enquiry to the responsible licensed employment agency. Ask which agency will receive your information and what it needs before sharing documents. The agency handles its own recruitment records and privacy practices.

WhatsApp and hosting

The contact link opens WhatsApp with a draft you can review before sending. WhatsApp handles that interaction under its own privacy policy. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. This site stores no enquiry details in your browser.

The site may store your language preference locally. Checklist selections are not sent to us. WhatsApp, GitHub and any agency you choose to deal with may handle data in other countries under their own terms and policies.

Retention and security

How long enquiry messages are retained depends on follow-up needs and applicable obligations. There is no published fixed retention period. Avoid sending sensitive documents in the initial chat and confirm the recipient and submission method with the licensed agency before sending documents.

Your privacy requests

You can ask about information you provided and request access, correction or deletion through the WhatsApp contact below. Applicable law and recordkeeping obligations can affect what can be provided or deleted. For information already held by a recruitment agency, you can also contact that agency directly.

Policy updates

When this policy changes, the current version and its version number will appear on this page.
