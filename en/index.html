Frequently asked questions

Opportunities

There are currently no verified vacancies listed. Ask what is available and request written details from the responsible licensed agency.

Candidate eligibility

Experience, qualifications, language ability and medical fitness requirements vary by role and destination law. Confirm the applicable criteria with the employer and licensed agency.

How to apply

Start with a general WhatsApp enquiry. Ask the agency to confirm whether it is accepting applications and which secure channel to use.

Fees and receipts

Pause if an advert promises a guaranteed job or visa, asks for payment into a personal account, or pressures you to act quickly. Verify fees and request an official receipt before paying. Keep receipts and messages. Never share OTPs or banking credentials. Report suspected fraud to your national employment authority.

Salary and benefits

Check basic pay and currency, working hours, overtime, deductions, accommodation, meals, transport, medical coverage, leave and contract duration in the written offer.

Visas and processing times

Steps and timing vary by job and country. The agency can explain the requirements applicable to you.

Document checklist

Tick what you have ready. The checks stay in this browser for the current visit. Ask the licensed agency which documents apply and how to submit them securely.

Licensed recruitment

Check the licence number and validity in the official register. Call the agency using the number listed there to confirm its representative and the job reference. Review the written contract before accepting.

Written employment contract

Ask for the employer’s details and written terms. Confirm duties, working hours, pay and any deductions with the agency before accepting.

Privacy Policy

You can ask about information you provided and request access, correction or deletion through the WhatsApp contact below. Applicable law and recordkeeping obligations can affect what can be provided or deleted. For information already held by a recruitment agency, you can also contact that agency directly.
