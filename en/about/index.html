About & Trust

Our aim is to make Gulf employment information easier to understand and help candidates verify who is responsible at each step.

How enquiries and referrals work

Information and referrals

GulfPath Overseas provides overseas employment information, explains the enquiry process and can refer interested candidates.

Licensed recruitment

The responsible licensed employment agency handles recruitment. Ask for the agency’s identity and verify its licence before proceeding.

Confirm written terms

Ask for the employer’s details and written terms. Confirm duties, working hours, pay and any deductions with the agency before accepting.

Privacy and expectations

Share only what is needed for your enquiry. Employment, visa approval and salary are subject to the formal process and carry no guarantee.

Licensed recruitment

Check the licence number and validity in the official register. Call the agency using the number listed there to confirm its representative and the job reference. Review the written contract before accepting.

  • Sri Lanka · SLBFE
  • Nepal · Department of Foreign Employment

Avoid recruitment fraud

Pause if an advert promises a guaranteed job or visa, asks for payment into a personal account, or pressures you to act quickly. Verify fees and request an official receipt before paying. Keep receipts and messages. Never share OTPs or banking credentials. Report suspected fraud to your national employment authority.

  • Sri Lanka · SLBFE
  • Nepal · Department of Foreign Employment (complaint@dofe.gov.np)
